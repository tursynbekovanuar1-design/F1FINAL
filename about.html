    About the Project

    Project Goal

    F1 Portal is an educational website about Formula 1. It brings together teams, drivers, race calendars and photos in one place, and shows live data from a public API.

    Target Audience

    Motorsport fans who want a quick overview of the season: casual viewers, new fans and students who follow the championship.

    What You Can Do Here

    Follow the Season

    Live race calendar, latest results and driver and constructor standings.

    Save Favourites

    Star your favourite drivers, filter photos and re-run recent searches. Everything is saved in your browser.

    Make It Yours

    Switch between light and dark mode, pick a background colour and use the site in English, Russian or Kazakh.

    How to Use the Portal

    1. Browse the Teams and Drivers pages for the current championship picture.
    2. Open Races to see the calendar and filter upcoming or completed races.
    3. Star drivers you like: they appear in the sidebar on every page.
    4. Create an account to see your profile and saved items.

    Built With

    • HTML5, CSS3 (Flexbox, Grid, media queries)
    • Bootstrap 5.3 (grid, navbar, cards, carousel, forms)
    • JavaScript and jQuery 3.7
    • localStorage (accounts, theme, favourites, filters)
    • Jolpica F1 API (live race data)
