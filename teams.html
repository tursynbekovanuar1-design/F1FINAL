      Frequently Asked Questions

      What is a Constructor's Championship?

      The World Constructors' Championship (WCC) is awarded by the FIA to the most successful F1 constructor over the course of the season, based on points accumulated by both team drivers.

      How are race points allocated?

      Points are awarded to the top 10 finishing drivers at each Grand Prix: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1. Since 2025 there is no extra point for the fastest lap.

      What are Power Unit regulations?

      F1 cars use 1.6-liter V6 turbocharged hybrid power units. From 2026 the electric side delivers about half of the total power, and cars run on advanced sustainable fuel.
